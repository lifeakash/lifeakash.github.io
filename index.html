Frequently Asked Questions

What makes GeoStamp Camera different from other GPS cameras?

GeoStamp Camera focuses on a clean user experience. While competing GPS camera apps bombard users with intrusive pop-up ads and video interruptions, GeoStamp uses non-intrusive banner ads that do not block your photo shooting workflow.

Can I customize the stamp templates?

Yes. You can customize the date and time format, coordinates, map view, compass heading, and altitude according to your documentation needs.

Does the camera work offline?

Yes. Photo capture, timestamps, and GPS coordinate tagging work completely offline using your phone's built-in hardware GPS sensor.
