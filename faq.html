Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to everything you need to know about GeoStamp Camera.

Is GeoStamp Camera completely free to download?

Yes. GeoStamp Camera is free to download and install from the Google Play Store, giving you instant access to GPS coordinates, live mini-maps, timestamps, and address watermarks.

Does GeoStamp Camera have intrusive pop-up ads?

No! Our primary design philosophy is a clean, professional user experience. GeoStamp Camera only displays subtle banner ads that never interrupt your camera shutter, block your screen, or force you to watch 30-second videos.

Does the camera work offline without mobile data or Wi-Fi?

Yes. Photo capture, timestamps, compass direction, altitude, and latitude/longitude coordinate stamping work 100% offline using your phone's internal GNSS/GPS hardware sensor.

Can I add my company logo to the photo stamps?

Yes. In the settings menu, you can upload your custom company, agency, or contractor logo to brand your photos for official inspection reports and client presentations.

Which coordinate formats are supported?

You can toggle between Decimal Degrees (DD, e.g. 27.050197° N, 88.266101° E), Degrees Minutes Seconds (DMS), UTM, and Open Location Codes (Plus Codes).

Can I tag specific project notes or site numbers?

Yes. GeoStamp includes a dedicated "Project Note / Site ID" feature that allows you to tag job numbers, building addresses, or inspection codes directly on each photo.
