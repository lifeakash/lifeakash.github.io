Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • AKD Studio

This Privacy Policy describes how AKD Studio ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information in the GeoStamp Camera mobile application (the "App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through transparent practices.

1. Information Collection and Permissions

To provide core GPS stamping and photographic verification features, the App requests the following permissions:

  • Location Information: Real-time geographic coordinates (latitude, longitude, altitude) via your device's built-in GNSS/GPS sensor. This data is processed strictly on-device to watermark your photos with addresses, coordinates, and mini-map overlays.
  • Camera & Media Access: Required to capture photos and save the stamped images directly to your local device storage.
  • Device Information: Non-personal device model and OS version information to display optional hardware verification (e.g., the "Captured by" signature).

2. Local On-Device Storage & Data Privacy

  • 100% Local Storage: All photos taken with GeoStamp Camera remain on your personal device. We do not upload, host, or store your photos on our servers.
  • No Location Tracking Database: We do not track, profile, or store your personal location history on any external servers or cloud databases.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

The App integrates trusted third-party SDKs for core functionality:

  • Google Play Services: Location provider and Google Maps integration.
  • Google AdMob: We display non-intrusive banner ads. AdMob may process non-sensitive device identifiers and IP addresses to serve compliant advertisements. GeoStamp Camera does not use invasive interstitial video popups during photo shooting.
  • OpenStreetMap & Yandex: Map and satellite tile imagery providers.

4. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

5. Security

We value your trust. Because all photos and location watermarks are processed and saved locally on your device, your images remain under your direct control without being transmitted across the internet.

6. Changes & Contact Us

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected directly on this page.
