About GeoStamp Camera

Built by AKD Studio, GeoStamp Camera was created to provide a modern, high-precision GPS photographic documentation tool for professionals who demand speed, accuracy, and an ad-free shutter experience.

🚫 Minimal Banner Ads

We strictly refuse to use intrusive 30-second video popups. Field engineers and inspectors can take photos continuously without delay.

📍 100% Hardware Precision

Your photos never leave your device. All watermarks and metadata are saved strictly on local storage without cloud harvesting.
